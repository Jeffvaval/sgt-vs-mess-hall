OUR STORY

The Mess Hall Mission

Sgt. V's Mess Hall is a small-batch home bakery where feminine charm meets a playful military twist. We craft oversized gourmet cookies with care, creativity, and plenty of personality.

Every batch is made to bring a little sweetness to your day. Welcome to the mess hall!

Pickup Logistics

Delivery Manifest
